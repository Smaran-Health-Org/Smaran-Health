Communication job
By the end, Indian specialist doctors should understand how Smaran Health could turn doctor-approved encounters into longitudinal patient memory, while recognizing what is prototyped, what remains product direction, and what must be proven in a controlled pilot.

Narrative arc
1. Begin with the follow-up experience doctors want.
2. Show why context is fragmented today.
3. Establish Smaran as a clinical-memory layer with doctor approval as the gate.
4. Walk one synthetic patient through the familiar clinic workflow.
5. Show existing prototype screens for consent, recording, review, approval, and memory.
6. Extend the vision into verified report/vital trends, approved WhatsApp handoff, and clinic follow-up visibility.
7. Frame continuity and practice impact as pilot hypotheses, not outcomes.
8. Close on trust gates and a doctor-led evidence pathway.

Visual system
- Canvas: 16:9, 1280 x 720.
- Palette: midnight ink #081A27, warm paper #F4F8F5, white #FFFFFF, mint #70D5AD, deep teal #155D66, electric blue #315FCD, coral #FF8A6B, muted slate #66736F.
- Typography: Avenir Next for editorial headings; Arial for body and UI labels.
- Motif: one continuous mint memory thread; coral marks uncertainty or an open loop.
- Status labels: SYNTHETIC PROTOTYPE, PRODUCT DIRECTION, PILOT HYPOTHESIS.
- Current prototype screens appear as screenshots; future screens are explicitly labelled concepts.
- Avoid robots, diagnostic language, promises of footfall growth, unsupported performance statistics, and dense dashboard grids.
